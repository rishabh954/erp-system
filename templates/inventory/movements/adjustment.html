Stock Adjustment

Manually increase, decrease, or set current inventory levels for a product

Adjustment Guidelines
1. Add Stock (+ Qty)

Adds the specified quantity to the current stock. Perfect for recording vendor delivery overages or found items.

2. Remove Stock (- Qty)

Deducts the specified quantity from the current stock. Used to record shrinkage, damage, theft, or items used internally.

3. Set Stock (= Qty)

Updates the stock quantity to the exact number entered, calculating the difference automatically. Typically used after a physical stock count.

Caution: Stock adjustments generate a permanent movement record and directly affect inventory valuation. Please verify counts before saving.
