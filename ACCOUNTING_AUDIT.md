# Accounting and finance audit

## Scope and evidence

This audit inspected the existing accounting models, services, views, URL/API surface, migrations, templates, tests, and direct accounting call sites in Sales, Purchase, Inventory, HRMS, Manufacturing, POS, and Assets. Evidence is based on the current repository implementation and executed tests, not on model/menu presence alone.

The existing accounting architecture is retained. The principal reusable components are `apps/accounting/models.py` (`Account`, `Journal`, `JournalEntry`, `JournalItem`, `BankAccount`, `BankStatement`, `TaxReturn`, `CostCenter`, `Budget`, `FixedAsset`, `RecurringJournalEntry`, `AuditLog`) and `apps/accounting/services.py` (`AutoJournalService`, `FinancialReportingService`, `AccountService`, `JournalEntryService`, `BankingService`).

## Verified implementation in this pass

`JournalEntry.post()` delegates to the reusable `AccountingPostingService` in `apps/accounting/services.py`. The service uses a transaction and row locks, validates draft status, journal/company match, fiscal-year/lock constraints, at least two nonzero lines, positive balanced totals, account ownership/activity/permissions, and cost-center ownership. It updates account balances and writes the existing `AuditLog` on successful posting. `JournalEntry.is_balanced()` aggregates line totals and checks non-empty positive totals against the entry header. `AccountingPostingService.reverse()` creates and posts an opposite entry linked to the posted original, requires a reason, preserves the original's posted status and lines, and writes a reversal audit event. It returns an existing posted reversal on retry. Posted journal entries and lines are protected at instance and queryset write boundaries, including bulk create/update and deletion; managers used for regular and unfiltered access retain those protections. Entry and line mutations lock their parent entry while checking its status. The existing trusted company-restore context explicitly bypasses these guards so a validated backup restore can replace tenant data and restore historical posted entries. `AutoJournalService` locks supported source rows and returns an existing posted entry for retries in sales invoice, credit-note, purchase bill, and sales/vendor payment methods; these are application-level guards, not database uniqueness guarantees.

The Balance Sheet and both Trial Balance calculation paths include opening balances effective as of the selected date and preserve balances from inactive accounts. The Balance Sheet includes cumulative net profit as retained earnings and displays it as a separate equity line. Account creation now posts a nonzero opening amount as a balanced general journal entry against a same-company equity offset, and the opening journal is visible in the GL and audit log. Reports detect the source journal and do not add the account's stored opening metadata a second time; legacy accounts without such an entry still use their stored value. AP aging groups eligible Bills by vendor and due-date buckets, subtracting completed payments dated on or before the report date. `ReportDateValidationMixin` validates `as_of`, `from_date`, and `to_date` parameters for account detail, Balance Sheet, Profit and Loss, Trial Balance, General Ledger, AR aging, and AP aging; malformed dates and invalid explicit ranges return HTTP 400. Reports still do not provide historical bill versioning, payment allocations, multi-currency conversion, or journalized audit history for pre-existing opening balances.

Focused regression coverage in `apps/accounting/test_posting_service.py` and `apps/accounting/test_regression_bugs.py` verifies opening-journal creation/posting/audit, debit- and credit-normal account sides, zero-balance behavior, no double-counting in the trial balance, invalid or cross-company offset rejection with rollback, and account-creation view integration. The accounting and purchase suites completed **84 passed**. Ruff, `git diff --check`, Django system checks, and migration drift checks passed. No schema change is required for the opening-journal workflow.

This is a foundation phase, not completion of the accounting specification. The 84-test accounting and purchase selection exercises those app suites only; it does not prove that HRMS, POS, or Manufacturing post accounting entries.

## Prioritized implementation roadmap

1. **Posting workflow and idempotency** — expose the reversal service in an authorized workflow and add replacement-entry handling; move retry-prone model save hooks behind explicit source posting; inspect existing duplicates before any unique constraint. Model/queryset immutability is implemented, but test admin/UI access and production-database locking. Tests: immutable entry/lines, balanced reversal, repeated and concurrent source posting, rollback.
2. **Ledger/report correctness** — batch-convert and reconcile legacy direct opening balances; add AP bill versioning/payment allocations and currency conversion; improve AR payment allocation and historical as-of behavior. Single-account audited opening entries, Balance Sheet retained earnings, effective opening balance display, bill/due-date AP aging, and accounting report date validation are implemented. Tests: reconciliation of every statement total to GL and historical aging boundaries.
3. **Sales and Purchase posting mappings** — use configurable account mappings and per-line revenue/expense/tax accounts, payment bank account, credits/debit notes, returns/refunds, and allocation handling. Tests: single/multi-line, multiple tax rates, partial/full/advance payment, retry.
4. **Bank reconciliation and cash management** — concurrency-safe matching, duplicate statement detection, configurable CSV mapping, reconciliation history/approved adjustments, cash/petty-cash reporting. Tests: duplicate and concurrent matching/import.
5. **Inventory accounting** — document and preserve current costing method; cover transfer, stock adjustment, scrap/write-off, return, and valuation-to-GL. Tests: inventory valuation equals control-account balance.
6. **HRMS, Manufacturing, POS integration** — payroll expense/liabilities and payment; WIP/finished goods/overhead/variance; POS tender split/refund/cash drawer. Integrate through the shared posting service. Tests: source workflow, GL balance, and retry idempotency.
7. **GST and reporting** — design configurable India GST rules and ledgers/reports after domain review; no hardcoded rates or statutory compliance claims. Add cash/bank book, cash flow, journal register, exports, filters, and query optimization.
8. **Database integrity and production verification** — validate/clean existing data before constraints; add justified company-aware uniqueness/check constraints and indexes; run concurrency tests on the production database, not SQLite alone.

## Migration and compatibility risks

The immutability change adds migration `apps/accounting/migrations/0007_alter_journalentry_options_alter_journalitem_options_and_more.py`, which records manager names and protected managers in migration state; it does not alter database columns or constraints. Future source uniqueness constraints may fail against existing duplicate references; inspect and reconcile data before migration. Immutability changes may affect current model-save hooks, fixtures, backup/restore, and administrative repair workflows. Opening-balance conversion must be a controlled migration/import with balanced offsets and a reconciliation report; do not overwrite existing current balances.

## Remaining production limitations

The system must not be described as enterprise-complete or GST-compliant yet. No PostgreSQL concurrency validation, opening-balance conversion, monthly close/reopen workflow, full reversal lifecycle, complete AR/AP allocation, statutory GST reports, financial export suite, or all-module accounting integrations have been verified.
